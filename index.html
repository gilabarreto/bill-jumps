About

A re-creation of Chrome’s popular offline game, Bill Jumps replaces the dinosaur with a familiar figure from an iconic 90s video. Press any key or click to leap over chairs and keep going for as long as you can. Simple, nostalgic, and fun, the game turns a memorable piece of tech history into a playful challenge.

Victor Barreto, a developer and retro gaming enthusiast, recreated and developed this game. Click here to learn more about him and explore his other projects.
